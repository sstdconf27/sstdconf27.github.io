### Scope

SSTD 2027 welcomes research that addresses the full spectrum of
challenges in managing and analyzing spatial and temporal data.

### Research Track

Authors are invited to submit **long and short research papers**
presenting original contributions.

## Topics of Interest

- Spatial and temporal data models
- Query processing and optimization
    - Indexing
    - Stream processing

## Submission Instructions

Submissions should be made at [the submission site](https://www.example.org/submit),
using the [ACM template](https://www.acm.org/publications/proceedings-template).
Long papers are limited to **10 pages**, short papers to **4 pages**.
