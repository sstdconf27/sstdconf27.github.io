## Scope

The 20th International Symposium on Spatial and Temporal Data (SSTD 2027)
continues the symposium's long and distinguished tradition as a premier forum
for researchers, practitioners, and industry leaders to present and discuss
innovations in spatial and temporal data management. Since its inception in
1989, SSTD has been at the forefront of advancing foundational theories,
systems, and applications that address the challenges of handling spatial and
temporal information.

Building on the expanded scope introduced in recent editions, SSTD 2027
embraces the full lifecycle of spatial and temporal data: from acquisition,
modeling, storage, and querying to analytics, mining, visualization, and
real-world deployment. The symposium particularly emphasizes the rapidly
evolving intersection of spatial and temporal data with artificial
intelligence, reflecting the growing importance of these data types in an era
of big data, foundation models, and ubiquitous computing. We invite
contributions spanning data management, analytics, mining, machine learning,
visualization, and applications across diverse domains, fostering
interdisciplinary discussions and accommodating emerging trends.

## Call for Contributions

SSTD 2027 solicits submissions in five tracks. All tracks are **single-blind**:
author names may appear on submissions, while reviewer names remain hidden
from authors.

### Research Track

Authors are invited to submit **regular research papers** presenting original
research contributions. The selection criteria include quality, novelty,
correctness, and relevance to SSTD.

Authors are also invited to submit **short research papers** that discuss
exciting new work not yet mature enough for a long paper. In particular, novel
but significant proposals will be considered for acceptance to this category
despite not having undergone extensive experimental validation or lacking a
strong theoretical foundation.

### Demo Track

Authors are invited to submit **demo papers** describing demonstrations of
systems, developed or under development. The selection criteria include the
novelty, the technical advances and challenges, and the overall practical
attractiveness of the demonstrated system.

### Industry Track

Authors are invited to submit **industry papers** that highlight practical
applications, address real-world challenges, share original insights, and
demonstrate the successful translation of research into impactful solutions
for businesses and organizations.

### Vision Track

Authors are invited to submit **vision papers** that describe revolutionary
ideas likely to guide research in the near future, challenge existing
assumptions prevalent in the research community, or identify novel
applications and technology trends that create new research challenges.

### Doctoral Student Forum

This event, which started at SSTD'25, provides a unique opportunity for
researchers in spatial and temporal data science and AI who are in the early
stages of their career to present their ongoing work and engage with more
experienced attendees from the broader academic and industrial communities.
This, in turn, will provide unique opportunities to receive constructive
feedback on their current research and build collaborative relationships.

## Two-Round Submission System (New for SSTD 2027)

For the first time in its history, SSTD 2027 will adopt a **two-round
submission system**. This change is designed to give authors greater
flexibility, provide earlier feedback, and accommodate the increasingly rapid
pace of research in spatial and temporal data science. Authors may choose to
submit to Round 1 or Round 2, or both, subject to the conditions below.

### Round 1 — Early Submissions (Only Research Track)

- **Submission deadline: December 6, 2026 (AoE)**
- Papers accepted in Round 1 will be considered for inclusion in the SSTD 2027
  proceedings and will have the opportunity to be revised before the
  camera-ready deadline.
- Authors of rejected papers in Round 1 may resubmit an improved version in
  Round 2.

### Round 2 — Regular Submissions (All Tracks)

- **Submission deadline: April 11, 2027 (AoE)**
- Round 2 is open to all authors, including those who did not submit in
  Round 1 and those resubmitting revised work following Round 1 decisions.
  Papers recommended in Round 1 for major revision are reviewed by the same PC
  members, whereas rejected papers will be considered as new submissions.
- All papers accepted across both rounds will be published in the SSTD 2027
  proceedings by ACM.

Papers submitted to Round 1 that are not accepted may be resubmitted to Round 2
with improvements, provided they comply with the standard originality and
dual-submission policies. Papers accepted in either round are expected to be
presented at the conference.

- Real-time spatio-temporal data streams for online and continual learning
- Human-in-the-loop AI systems leveraging spatial and temporal context

## Journal Special Issue

Outstanding research papers will be selected for an extended journal
submission to a special track of "SSTD 2027 Best Papers" in *GeoInformatica*
(Springer) and/or *ACM Transactions on Spatial Algorithms and Systems* (TSAS).
One paper will be selected for the **"SSTD's Ralf H. Güting Best Paper
Award."**

## Submission Instructions

Submissions should be formatted using the double-column
[ACM SIG Conference Proceedings template](http://www.acm.org/publications/proceedings-template).

If you use Overleaf, you can access the
[ACM official template](https://www.overleaf.com/gallery/tagged/acm-official).
This template requires switching the document class from "manuscript" to
"sigconf" to ensure the required double-column format.

Submissions cannot have been or be submitted to another venue while being
reviewed at SSTD and must have significant new content compared to previous
publications.

| Track | Page Limit |
|-------|------------|
| Regular Research Papers | 10 pages (excluding references) |
| Industry Papers | 10 pages (excluding references) |
| Short Research Papers | 4 pages (excluding references) |
| Demo Papers | 4 pages (excluding references) |
| Vision Papers | 4 pages (excluding references) |

Papers should be submitted via the CMT submission system (link TBD) to the
appropriate track and round. The review process is single-blind: reviewers are
not identified, while author names are listed in the submission.

All accepted papers will be published in the SSTD 2027 proceedings by ACM.

The fair use of AI tools is permitted and must comply with the ACM policies
regarding AI tools in authorship and the peer-reviewing process. For more
information, please see
[https://www.acm.org/publications/policies](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies).

### Important Note to Authors About the ACM Open Access Publishing Model

ACM has introduced a new open access publishing model for the International
Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS). Authors based at institutions that are
not yet part of the ACM Open program and do not qualify for a waiver will be
required to pay an article processing charge (APC) to publish their ICPS
article in the ACM Digital Library. To determine whether an APC will be
applicable to your article, please follow the
[detailed guidance here](https://www.acm.org/publications/icps/author-guidance).

Further information may be found on the ACM website:

- [Full details of the new ICPS publishing model](https://www.acm.org/publications/icps/faq)
- [Full details of the ACM Open program](https://www.acm.org/publications/openaccess)

Please direct all questions about the new model to
[icps-info@acm.org](mailto:icps-info@acm.org).
